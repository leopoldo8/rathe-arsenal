# Close the deck: persona panel, round 1 (2026-10-21)

Six simulated personas (fresh agents, each given only its own profile, its staging journey, today's screens and the mockup PNGs) reviewed alternatives A, B and C from `BRIEF.md`.
Order was shuffled per persona, but the blind labels leaked: every PNG carries the caption "Alternativa X". Letters carry no preference, so rankings are kept.
These are simulations built from research, not user evidence; each persona listed which claims it invented.

No structure wins: each is first twice. The reasons converge on parts, not on a whole.

## Where they agree

1. The number that matters is the total after what I own and the swaps I accept (B's "R$ 423,48"), but nobody wants a mandatory wizard to get it (Bia, Marcos reject forced steps; Caio fears "Pular" silently inflating the total). -> a live plan summary on top of a list.
2. A single "Comprar na Cúpula DT · R$ 724,48" button reads as buying the whole deck (Caio, Bia).
3. "A loja não tem" and "ainda não consultada" must stay separate everywhere, including the group text (Caio, Marcos, Duda). Bia asks why the app does not simply know the price.
4. The group message must carry the short list itself (what the store lacks), with pitch and foil flags; the friend view "Você tem N · Oferecer" turns asking into matching (Caio, Marcos, Bia keep it).
5. "Só foil promo" and "2 de 3 na loja" flags are valued by sellers and store owners; Marcos wants the regular printing, not the promo foil.
6. Jargon blocks the beginner and the parent: "Fechar o deck" (sounds like deleting), "coleção cobre 2 de 3", "Tenho...", "orçamento", "foil", "procura 3", "não consultadas".

## Where they conflict

- **Public page, parent vs other store owner.** Dona Marta wants a short page to buy at Cúpula DT within a budget. Renata reads any Cúpula DT price, "Comprar" or "envia para todo o Brasil" as a storefront for her competitor and a price ceiling; she would answer a neutral list, and use the quote mode (C) only if the customer never sees Cúpula DT there.
- **"Comprei" on the parent's phone only** creates a duplicate-purchase fear (Marta): the owner never sees it.
- **What to show first for a beginner.** Bia's real job is "the 5 cheap cards worth buying", not "complete the deck"; Marta wants "what is essential" within R$ 200.

## Owner decisions after round 1 (2026-10-21)

1. Direction (proposed by Claude, not yet approved by the owner): one missing list with a live summary on top (total after "Tenho" and accepted swaps), inline "Tenho" with quantity and undo, inline swap with savings, per-row "Comprar" link; no mandatory steps. The deck page shows a summary that opens the list.
2. Prices are looked up automatically, with separate freshness for price and availability:
   - Price (with quantity and printing) comes from the product page (about 5 Firecrawl credits per card) and stays valid for 30 days; the screen shows its date.
   - Availability comes from a periodic pass over the store's in-stock search (`txt_estoque=1`, about 150 credits for the whole catalog). A card missing from that pass becomes "a loja não tem agora" without a per-card lookup.
   - Cadence starts weekly (fits the free plan). The cadence and the price validity must be configuration, not code, so they can change later without a deploy.
   - Cards never found on the store site say "não achamos no site da Cúpula DT" with a link to search there.
3. Public pages: one address per intent. "For someone to buy for me" shows Cúpula DT prices and "Comprar" links (the parent case). "Ask a store for a quote" is a neutral list without Cúpula DT, where the store owner fills quantity and price and answers by WhatsApp. This adds a quote intent to `share_link`.
   - Hard requirement from the owner: the label of the parent option must not read as "someone wants to buy my cards" (selling), which is what usually happens in this community. Round 2 tests label comprehension before showing descriptions.
