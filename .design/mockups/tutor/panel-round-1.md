# Tutor: persona panel, round 1 (2026-10-22)

Two simulated personas compared alternatives A (the number in words, the Tutor decides it; questions next to each problem in the checklist) and B (a quantity and a source the player changes on the card; the deck's Tutor tile with a first question that follows the deck's state), shuffled per persona, no captions, with today's screens (`p3-13`, `p3-14`, `p3-16`, `p3-19`).
Picks are mapped from what they described, not from the proposal number (keys in the session scratchpad, `j6/keys.json`; responses in `j6/responses/r1-P*.md`).
These are simulations built from research, not user evidence.

## Where they agree

1. **The proposal in words, no counter** (both): A.
2. **Say why the card that comes in is good, not only that it is legal** (both): "Eu pedi boa e barata; o Tutor só diz carta legal para o seu Rhinar."
3. **"você não tem" is ambiguous** (Marcos): in the pool, or in the collection? If he owns the card, he would pay for one he has.

## Problems found by one persona, worth keeping

- Show what the swap does to the missing cards (Marcos): "Faltam" goes from 7 to 8.
- After applying, go back to the deck with the new card marked and "Desfazer" (Bia). "Desfazer" exists today ("Troca feita no deck." with "Desfazer") and was not drawn.
- For a Blitz deck short of cards, the new card goes into the deck, not the inventory (Bia, reading Marcos's Classic Constructed screen).
- "Conversar sobre este deck" reads the same as the three questions (Bia).

## Prompt-shaped caveats

- Only two personas; both were told the Tutor's answers are already right, so neither judged the answer's correctness.
- Each saw the other's scenario on one of the two screens (Marcos read Bia's Blitz deck page; Bia read Marcos's Classic Constructed proposal).
