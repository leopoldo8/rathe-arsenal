## Direction from round 1 (approved by the owner, 2026-10-22)

- **Editor**: B's problems on each card, "+" disabled at the limit, and a bar that stays visible while scrolling ("31 de 40 · 1 problema"); the bar opens A's checklist, with what is right in green as well as what is wrong (Bia, Marcos).
- **Inventory**: two counts per card, "No deck" and "No inventário" (B, Marcos), and "Faltam" counting both with the split in words: "Faltam 7 cartas (4 do deck, 3 do inventário)" (A; Lucas, Marcos, Bia).
- **Getting the list out**: "Copiar lista" and "Duplicar" as visible buttons on the deck page, "Duplicar" away from "Excluir" (B; Lucas, Caio); "Copiar lista" opens a sheet with the two tabs and the text preview before copying (C; Bia, Caio, Renata, Marcos).
- **Delete**: "Excluídos recentemente", kept 30 days, with "Desfazer" until the next action (B; Bia, Caio, Marcos).
- **Shared deck**: A's verdict block ("Blitz · ainda não pode jogar" with each problem in plain words and "o deck tem 31 de 40") as what the visitor sees first, and B's format chips under it to check another format (Lucas, Caio, Marcos); Bia warned the chips could make Renata think the deck is fine in another format.
- **New deck**: hero first (s1a, all three who had the task), with s1b's one-line explanation per format, and no hero pre-selected (Marcos: the first result looked chosen).

Decided by the owner with the direction (2026-10-22): the inventory copies count in the missing cards, the shopping list and the copies a deck holds against spare (journey 5), always split in words as deck and inventory.

## Where they agree

1. **"Faltam" means two things** (Renata, Caio, Lucas, Bia): "Faltam 9 cartas" for the deck size reads as "she must buy 9", because elsewhere "Faltam" is what the player does not own. The deck size needs its own words.
2. **Say what to do, not only what is wrong** (Renata, Caio, Lucas): Renata wants "Tire 1 Pack Hunt (Vermelho) e coloque mais 10 cartas" with the arithmetic done (removing the extra copy makes the deck 30, not 31); Caio wants one tap to keep 1 copy of each of the 8 cards instead of 8 taps on "-".
3. **"Pelas regras atuais" says neither what changed nor when** (all five). The official change log dates it: "9 March 2026, 7.3 Blitz Format: Updated to remove living legend rule and reduce max copies of each card to 1" (`https://fabtcg.com/rules-and-policy-center/change-log-tournament-rules-and-policy/`, read 2026-10-22).
4. **The deck status contradicts the verdict** (Marcos, Caio, Renata): "Pronto" or "Jogando" next to "Não pode mais jogar no Blitz" or a red problem; Marcos stops trusting "Pronto".
5. **Show the result before switching format** (Lucas, Marcos): "Mudar para Living Legend" should say first whether the deck becomes valid there ("Em Living Legend: válido", or the problems left). Bia did not know whether her Armory accepts Living Legend.
6. **Unknown words**: "Card pool" (Lucas, Caio, Bia; Marcos uses it), "Aposentar" (Caio, Bia, Marcos), "jovem" (Caio, Bia), "Inventário" (Bia). Journey 7 owns jargon; here each needs one plain line where it appears.

## Problems found by one persona, worth keeping

- **The export says nothing when the pool is illegal** (Marcos): he would register Pack Hunt 3 in the deck plus 1 in the inventory. Warn in the export sheet, without blocking.
- **He will not drop GEM until a list exported from here imports there** (Marcos). The format is still a hand check.
- **"Mover para o inventário" does not say how many** (Marcos): one copy or all three.
- **The checklist sheet covers half the list on the phone** (Bia), and a sheet that closes on scroll loses the warning (Marcos): the bar stays, the sheet is opened on purpose.
- **The rule-change notice exists only inside the app** (Marcos). Notifications are out of this journey.
- **The verdict must show without an account** (Renata): the shared deck page is public.
- **The shared text list should carry "Copiar", the missing marks and the problems** (Marcos).
- **"As trocas de cartas" in the delete warning** (Caio) means the replacements; he read it as trades.
