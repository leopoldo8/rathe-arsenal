These are simulations built from research, not user evidence. Responses are in the session scratchpad (`j3/responses/r2-P*.md`).

## Solved, as the personas quoted the screens

- "Faltam" with two meanings: the deck size now reads "O deck tem 31 de 40 cartas" and "coloque mais 8"; "Faltam 7 cópias de 4 cartas · 4 do deck, 3 do inventário" is about buying (Renata, Caio, Marcos).
- The fix comes with the arithmetic: "Para jogar no Blitz: tirar 1 Pack Hunt (Vermelho) e colocar mais 10 cartas." (Renata).
- The rule change says what and when: "Desde 9 de março de 2026, o Blitz permite 1 cópia de cada carta (antes eram 2)." (all who saw it).
- "Deixar 1 de cada" replaces 8 taps (Caio, Bia).
- The result before switching: "Em Living Legend este deck é válido" (Lucas, his top change).
- The illegal export warns without blocking; "Mover 1" and "Mover todas (3)" say how many; the text list shows "faltam N" and the problem on the line (Marcos).
- Delete is reversible: "Restaurar", "Um deck excluído volta com as cartas, as substituições e o histórico." (Caio, Bia).

## Where they agree (to fix in round 3)

1. **The stage still contradicts the verdict** (Bia, Caio, Marcos, Lucas, Renata): "Pronto · 1 problema" and "Jogando · não pode jogar no Blitz". Putting both on one line did not solve it. This needs an owner pick between two ways to show it (round 3 draws both).
2. **"Para o grupo" was never shown** (Caio, Lucas, Bia): only the English tab was drawn, so nobody knows whether the Portuguese text carries the inventory or how it separates it. Lucas fears a friend reads the deck and the inventory as one deck.
3. **"jogo 1" in the inventory line means nothing** to a returning or new player (Lucas, Bia).
4. **The format chips on the shared page show no result** (Marcos), and Bia expects Renata to tap them.

## Problems found by one persona, worth keeping

- "Card pool: 35 de 52" is still jargon for a beginner, even with "o herói não conta" (Bia).
- "coloque mais 9" in the editor and "colocar mais 10" on the shared page read as a mismatch unless the step is written (Bia).
- No way to send the deck link from the editor drawn (Bia); sharing exists today (shipped 2026-10-18) but was not on the screen.
- "Copiar lista" on the shared page does not say where it copies to (Renata).
- "Deixar 1 de cada" removes without letting him choose (Caio, Marcos); after it the deck is 32 of 40 and nothing suggests what to add (Caio).
- "Duplicar" next to "Copiar lista" is not understood (Caio).
- After "Mudar para Living Legend" no screen shows the result, so he trusts it only after reopening (Lucas).
- "Desde 9 de março de 2026" should link the announcement (Marcos).

## Kept as decided, not changed

- No date or history for a hero outside a format (Lucas asked when Kayo stopped being allowed): copy rule 4, the card data holds no date.
- The GEM format stays a hand check before building (Marcos, Renata, Bia).

## Asked for, outside this journey

- Version and foil per line in the text list (Marcos): journey 2's version model.
- Two dates on the missing list, prices and stock (Marcos): journey 4's copy.
