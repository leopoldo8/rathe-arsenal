# Getting an account: persona panel, round 1 (2026-10-22)

Four simulated personas compared alternatives A (e-mail and password, fixed), B (a code by e-mail, no password) and C (Google first, e-mail second), shuffled per persona, no captions, with today's screens (audit screenshots `p1-01`, `p1-03`, `p1-04`, `p1-05`, `p1-06`, `p1-07`, `p6-01`, `p7-01`, `p7-02`).
Each persona picked per part. Picks below are mapped from what they described, not from the proposal number they wrote (keys in the session scratchpad, `j1/keys.json`; responses in `j1/responses/r1-P*.md`).
These are simulations built from research, not user evidence.

## Picks per part

| Part | Bia (P1) | Lucas (P5) | Dona Marta (P6) | Renata (P7) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Arriving, what the app does | B (one field) | B | C (its screen named Pedro's list) | not her task |

## Where they agree

1. **Coming back without a password** (all four): "o e-mail já vem preenchido e não há senha para esquecer". This is B's strongest part.
2. **The typo messages before sending** (all four): "Falta o final do e-mail, por exemplo bia@gmail.com" and "faltam 4" replace today's "Aguarde 58 minutos". B drew no typo screen; every combined design needs them on the e-mail field.
3. **A short code fails the real case** (Bia, Marta, Renata): Bia opens the e-mail on Saturday, and 15 minutes or even 24 hours have passed. Renata at the counter: 15 minutes "vence rápido". A's expired screen, which already sent a new link with no typing, won.
4. **Saying where you go back to** (Bia, Lucas, Renata, Marta): "Depois de entrar, você volta para o deck da Bia" is what makes the shared-link sign-up worth it.

## Problems found by one persona, worth keeping

- **Use the app right away, confirm later** (Bia's top change): "Usar o app logo depois de digitar o e-mail e confirmar no sábado. Hoje e nas três propostas o código ou link vence antes de eu abrir o e-mail."
- **Stay signed in on this phone** (Renata's top change): "Manter conectado neste celular, para não repetir o código toda semana." Today a session lasts 7 days.
- **Show how you got in last time** (Lucas): C's "Da última vez você entrou com e-mail e senha" next to B's single field.
- **"formato do torneio" means nothing to a parent** (Marta); the pitch speaks to players. From a shared link, the reason for being there ("guardar a lista do Pedro") matters more than the pitch.
- **"Olhe também o spam e a aba Promoções"** only fits Gmail (Renata, store address).
- **"Mandamos um código" does not say it creates the account** (Bia).
- **"Sua conta do Google foi ligada à conta que você já tinha"** confused Marta, who did not know she had another account.
- **The pitch does not say the app warns when a hero leaves a format** (Lucas).

## Asked for, outside this journey

- Pulling the store's stock into the app (Renata): journey 2 (getting cards in) and journey 5 (selling).

## Prompt-shaped caveats

- Simulated personas lean toward the fewest taps; B and C read as shorter partly for that reason. The cost of B and C (codes stored for a short time; a Google sign-in with account linking) is the owner's to weigh.
- Only C drew Marta's own shared link (Pedro's list); the other two drew Bia's deck, so Marta's picks for parts A and B follow her scenario, not the design.
- The mockup convention "Você tocou em ..." confused Lucas and Renata on A's expired screen ("eu não toquei"); the next round draws states without it where the screen already says what happened.
- Each persona was told its own task again, which steers what it looked at.
