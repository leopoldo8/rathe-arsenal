# Sell and trade: round 2 brief (the combined design)

Round 1 (`BRIEF.md`, `panel-round-1.md`) compared A, B and C per part. This brief draws one combined design, approved by the owner as a direction on 2026-10-21. The scenario, shared rules and owner decisions in `BRIEF.md` still hold, with the changes listed here.

## Changes from round 1

- **Spare is the player's rule.** Before any spare number shows, the app asks once "Você passa cartas de um deck para outro?" with three options, none selected: "Sim, entre decks do mesmo formato" / "Sim, entre quaisquer decks" / "Não, cada deck tem as próprias cartas". Duda's scenario numbers use the first option. The word "seguram" is gone everywhere: copies "ficam nos decks".
- **Nothing is pre-selected** in the spare page, the trade offer, or the "Em troca procuro" row. Screens show the state after the persona's taps, and each such screen says so in its own words ("Você escolheu 9 cópias").
- **A version the app does not know is never posted as known.** Before sending, lines with no version that exist in foil ask once, together: "3 linhas sem versão informada (Enlightened Strike, Snatch, Pummel). São sem foil?" with "Sim, todas sem foil" and "Ver uma por uma". After "Sim", those lines read "sem foil". A card that has no foil printing in the catalog asks nothing and shows no finish. The builder checks each card's finishes in the catalog and reports the final message text.
- **The store price is shown next to the suggestion** (from `store_stock`, which the collection value already uses): "Sugerido R$ 6 · Cúpula DT a partir de R$ 1,50". Illustrative Cúpula DT prices: Enlightened Strike R$ 8,00, Command and Conquer R$ 2,50, Snatch R$ 1,50, Pummel R$ 0,90, Head Jab R$ 0,50, Pack Hunt R$ 0,30, Wounding Blow R$ 0,20. The price helper line stays: "Sugerido: TCGplayer em dólar × 7. O 7 cobre o dólar e o custo de trazer a carta; mude se você cobra diferente." No "Ver na LigaFAB" link (Renata and Duda read it as one more step per line; the store price answers the same doubt).
- **The closing line is never borrowed.** First time: an empty field with the grey example "Ex.: Aceito PIX · retirada no centro ou envio pelos Correios". After that: the seller's own last line, shown in full, editable, with no "da última vez" label. Duda's line: "Aceito PIX · retirada na loja do centro ou envio pelos Correios".
- **Links are real-looking**: `https://rathearsenal.app/l/k7Qm2x` (Duda's sell list), `https://rathearsenal.app/l/p3Rv8n` (Caio's "Procuro - Rhinar").
- **Caio's want list "Procuro - Rhinar"**: 2 Wrecker Romp (Azul) at a suggested R$ 3 each and 1 Savage Feast (Vermelho) at R$ 15. Total R$ 21. Verify both cards and pitches in the catalog; if a pitch does not exist, pick another Brute card that does and report it.
