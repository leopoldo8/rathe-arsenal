## Problems found in round 2 (design and copy, no owner decision needed)

1. **The store price next to a foil or a costly printing misleads** (Caio, Lucas, Duda): "Sugerido R$ 60 · Cúpula DT a partir de R$ 8,00" compares a Rainbow Foil with the cheapest printing the store sells, and Lucas read it as what the store pays. Fix: say what it is, "A Cúpula DT vende a versão mais barata por R$ 8,00", and show it only on rows with no version or a regular version; never next to a foil or a named costly printing.
2. **"Ver na LigaFAB" was missed** (Duda, Marcos): both price on LigaFAB and the store price does not replace it. Fix: bring it back on the seller's own screens only (never on the public page, where Renata objected to sending the buyer away).
3. **The rule question shows no effect and asks people it does not concern** (Lucas has one deck; Marcos wants the number before choosing; Renata read it as a barrier). Fix: ask only when the player has two decks that hold copies of the same card (otherwise every option gives the same count, and nothing is asked); each option shows its result ("Sobram 20 cópias" / "24" / "19").
4. **"Sim, todas sem foil" is one tap for cards nobody checked** (Duda, Marcos), and the screen showed it already answered, which read as automatic. Fix: draw the question before the answer; the three cards are listed with their own "Sem foil / Foil / Não sei"; "Sim, todas sem foil" stays as a shortcut under the list. "Não sei" keeps the line out of the message's finish ("foil não conferido") and keeps "preço aproximado".
5. **"Preço aproximado" disappeared from the draft** (Lucas): "sem foil" does not settle edition. Fix: lines whose version is not fully known keep "preço aproximado" in grey on the seller's screen.
6. **Pre-set quantities still read as the app's choice** (Lucas, Marcos, Duda). Fix: draw the spare page in its first state (all at 0, bar "Escolha o que vender") as its own screen, then the state after Duda's taps.
7. **"Manter na coleção" is not understood** (Renata, Marcos). Fix: "Sim, tirar 2 da coleção" / "Não, só marcar como vendida", with "Escolha não se essas cópias não estavam no app."
8. **The trade value line reads as "this trade is unfair"** (Caio). Fix: "Valor aproximado, pelo preço sugerido: você oferece R$ 57 · você procura R$ 21", with no comparison word, and the line stays off the message.
9. **The offer message drops finish and condition** (Marcos). Fix: the offer lines carry what the app knows, like the sell message ("3x Pummel (Vermelho) · sem foil").
10. **An Ideia deck in testing frees its cards** (Marcos): the notice exists but offers nothing. Fix: the notice gets "Marcar Dash ideia como Montando", which moves those 3 Head Jab back into the deck's copies.
11. **The back link "‹ Procuro - Kayo" on the offer message** (Caio) reads like his own list. Fix: "‹ Lista do Marcos".
12. **The list name is fixed** (Renata): "Vendo - 21/10" is editable on the draft.
